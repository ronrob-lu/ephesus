# 🏛️ Ephesus

> **Bring the timeless grandeur of classical antiquity to Luanti & Minetest.**  
> Handcrafted 16px Greco-Roman architectural blocks, authentic 3D marble columns, decorative capitals, imperial mosaics, and Mediterranean flora.

---

## ✨ Features

Whether you are erecting a grand Roman forum, a hilltop Greek pantheon, a lavish Mediterranean villa, or an ancient amphitheater, **Ephesus** provides the architectural toolkit to turn your vision into reality.

### 🏛️ 3D Architectural Meshes & Classical Columns
* **Large Columns & Capitals:** Majestic fluted columns (`marble_column`) with ornate column caps (`hat_column`).
* **Medium Columns & Capitals:** Slender architectural pillars (`hat_medium`) with matching caps (`medium_column_top`).
* **Cornices, Socles & Architraves:** Classical pedestals (`pedestal`), upper corner cornices (`hat_angle`), wall tops (`hat`), and structural braces (`braces`).
* **Architectural Precision Slabs:** Specially tuned 15/16 slabs and corner slabs for detailed friezes and pediments.
* **Brick Slabs:** Fine decorative stone slabs and corners.

### 🏺 Noble Building Materials & Mosaics
* **Classical Marbles:** Pure White Marble, Polished Marble, and Grey Marble.
* **Imperial Mosaics:** Vibrant Roman Blue and Red geometric mosaics.
* **Terracotta Tiles:** Traditional Mediterranean roof and patio tiles.

### 🌿 Mediterranean Woods & Foliage
* **Cypress:** Slender, stately trees with unique bark textures, timber planks, and 3 distinct foliage density variations.
* **Olive & Apricot:** Rich Mediterranean orchard woods, decorative trunk blocks, and lush leaves.
* **Fig:** Dense, verdant foliage.

## 🎮 Universal Compatibility

**Ephesus is fully multi-game ready!** It works out-of-the-box across your favorite Luanti / Minetest games with zero configuration:

| Game | Status | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **VoxeLibre** *(formerly MineClone 2)* | ✅ Native | Full support with `pickaxey` / `axey` / `sheary` digging, `mcl_sounds`, creative tabs, and `mcl_stairs`. |
| **Mineclonia** | ✅ Native | Seamless Minecraft-style mechanics, tool wear, and soundscapes. |
| **Minetest Game (MTG)** | ✅ Native | Classic MTG tool groups (`cracky`, `choppy`, `snappy`) and standard sounds. |

### 🧩 Recommended & Optional Mods
* **[i3](https://content.minetest.net/packages/jp/i3/):** Enhanced next-gen craft guide and inventory. Ephesus includes built-in item compression to keep your recipe book clean and organized.
* **[moreblocks / stairsplus](https://content.minetest.net/packages/Calinou/moreblocks/):** Unlocks full circular-saw support: slopes, microblocks, panels, and trim for all Ephesus stone and wood types!

## 📦 Installation

### Via ContentDB (Recommended)
1. Open Luanti / Minetest.
2. Go to the **Content** tab.
3. Search for **Ephesus** and click **Install**.

### Manual Installation
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Extract the folder into your Luanti `mods/` directory:
   * **Linux:** `~/.minetest/mods/ephesus`
   * **Windows:** `<minetest_install_dir>\mods\ephesus`
   * **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/minetest/mods/ephesus`
3. Enable the mod in your world configuration menu!
